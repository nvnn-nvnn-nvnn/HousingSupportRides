The paperwork to get housed is its own full-time job: waitlists, income
verifications, inspections, and forms that each ask for the same thing in a
slightly different way. Most people navigate it alone. Maria didn't have to.

## One thread, held

For nine months, the same coordinator sat beside her through every step —
translating agency-speak, making the follow-up calls, and driving her to the
appointments that kept the application moving.
