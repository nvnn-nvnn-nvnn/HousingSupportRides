Replace this paragraph with the opening. Lead with a person or a moment rather
than a summary — who showed up, what the weather did, what the first thing you
noticed was. Two or three sentences.

## What happened

The shape of the day: where it was held, roughly how many people came, who
organized it, what there was to eat, what people did. If certificates or
milestones were recognized, that belongs here.

## Why it matters

Connect the day back to the work. A picnic is not a program, but showing up for
each other outside of a crisis is the part of recovery that services cannot
provide. Say that in your own words.

## Thank you

Name the volunteers, donors, and partner organizations who made it possible —
once you have confirmed each of them is happy to be named.
