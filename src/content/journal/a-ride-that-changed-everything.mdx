James had been out of work for fourteen months. Not for lack of trying — he had
the certifications, two solid references, and an interview lined up across town.
What he did not have was a car, and the bus that would get him there did not run
early enough.

## One morning, one ride

A volunteer driver picked him up at 7:40. He walked in fifteen minutes early,
calm instead of frantic. He got the job.

> The interview was never really the hard part. Getting to it was.

## Why a ride is never just a ride

- It's showing up on time to the appointment that renews your benefits.
- It's making the court date that keeps your record clean.
- It's the first day of work — and the second, and the thirtieth.
