**October 2026 - Vang New Year Event Set up**&#x20;

Housing Support Rides had the opportunity to give back to the community, and assist with the event set up at a major Hmong-American event in Minnesota; \
\
**Noj Tsiab Celebration — 2026 - Vang Pre-New Year Celebration by the Vang Council of Minnesota**&#x20;

---

On October 3rd, 2026, our members contributed to the event set up, and were responsible for the coordination of decor for the event.&#x20;

We are grateful for the opportunity to contribute to this event, and to support our community, strengthen our cultural bonds, and assist with the organization of events in partnership with the **Vang Council of Minnesota** .\
\
Information about the 2026-2027 Vang New Year

The **Noj Tsiab Celebration** is a celebration hosted by the **Vang Council of Minnesota**&#x20;

This pre-new year cultural event honors tradition, community, and the spirit of unity within the Vang family and the broader Hmong community.

Located within the monolithic Aldrich Arena, at 1960 White Bear Ave, in Maplewood Minnesota, Housing Support Rides organization, and members had the responsibility of event decor, setup, and communication.&#x20;

\
Our members prepared the entire arena for an estimated 500+ guests -&#x20;

We were responsible for handling:

* Decoration
* Event Handling
* Interior Design&#x20;
* Communications Coordination&#x20;
