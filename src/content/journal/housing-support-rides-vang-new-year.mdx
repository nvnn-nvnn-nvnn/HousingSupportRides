# **October 2026: Vang Pre-New Year Event Setup**

Housing Support Rides partnered with one of Minnesota's major Hmong-American cultural events;&#x20;

The **Noj Tsiab Celebration**, hosted by the Vang Council of Minnesota.

\
**Overview**\
On October 3, 2026, our members helped set up the Noj Tsiab Celebration at Aldrich Arena, located at 1960 White Bear Ave, in Maplewood, MN. We prepared the arena for an estimated 500+ guests and were responsible for:

* Decoration and interior design
* Event setup and day-of handling
* Communications coordination with the Vang Council of Minnesota

**About the event**\
The Noj Tsiab Celebration is a pre-New Year cultural event for the 2026–2027 Vang New Year. It honors tradition, community, and unity within the Vang family and the wider Hmong community.

**Why it mattered**\
We are grateful to have supported our community, strengthened our cultural bonds, and partnered with the Vang Council of Minnesota on an event of this size.\
\
Importantly, we are glad to be able to have our own members assist the community, and contribute to the organization of the event. &#x20;

---

**\
Event Organization & Set Up**
-----------------------------

Housing Support Rides team arrived at the Aldrich Arena in the early morning to set up the event by first in foremost organize and cleaning everything up.&#x20;



![](/img/journal/housing-support-rides-vang-new-year/VangNewYear_001.jpg)

The first thing that we needed to do before setting everything up for the event was by emptying out the venue within the arena.\
\
We needed to prioritize creating an empty space within the arena to first implement everything in order:

1. Setting up the tables, chairs, and designated communal areas&#x20;
2. Setting up the main stage for the main leaders
3. Decorating the event with the appropriate attire



We had about a dozen members within the Housing Support Rides team, and so we sectioned off and split our group into 3 teams responsible for each setup responsibility.
