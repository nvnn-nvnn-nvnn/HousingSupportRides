Most discharge permits are boring on purpose. They arrive as dense PDFs, they
use the same boilerplate, and they assume nobody will read past the summary.

We read past the summary.

## The number that didn't fit

Buried in an appendix was a proposed thermal limit that would have let the
facility return water **four degrees warmer** than the stretch below it could
tolerate in late summer. Four degrees is the difference between a cold-water
fishery and a warm one.

## Eighteen months

Contesting a permit is not glamorous. It is comment letters, public hearings,
and a great deal of waiting. But the science was clear, the monitoring data was
ours, and the record we had built over years of sampling is what carried it.

The revised permit holds the temperature line. The river below it still runs
cold.
