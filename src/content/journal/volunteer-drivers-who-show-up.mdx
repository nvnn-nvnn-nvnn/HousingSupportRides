The first ride of the day leaves before sunrise. By 6am, four volunteer drivers
are already out — coffees in the cupholder, routes on the passenger seat.

## An ordinary morning

They aren't doing anything dramatic. They're getting a night-shift worker home,
a mom to a clinic, a young man to the first day of a job. Ordinary trips that
would otherwise not happen.

> You don't need a special skill. You need a car and a couple of mornings.
