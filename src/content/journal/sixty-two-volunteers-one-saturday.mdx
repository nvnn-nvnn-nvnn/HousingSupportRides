We expected forty people. Sixty-two showed up, most of them before the coffee
was even out of the truck.

## The haul

By two in the afternoon the tally was **3.4 tons** across a two-mile stretch —
our largest single-day cleanup on record. The usual suspects were there: tires,
bottles, a shopping cart with a story we chose not to ask about.

But the thing that stays with you isn't the weight. It's what the weight means:
every pound of it was sitting in the river last week, and it isn't now.

## Why we feed everyone

People ask why a small nonprofit spends money on lunch for volunteers. The
answer is simple — the people who came out on a cold Saturday are the same
people who will come out next month, and the month after. Showing up is the
whole program.
